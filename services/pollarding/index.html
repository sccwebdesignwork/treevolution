What pollarding can involve.

Pollarding is a particular management method and should not be treated as a generic solution for every tree. Where it is appropriate, a planned approach helps manage recurring growth and future maintenance.

Maintaining an established pollarding cycle
Managing recurring growth in appropriate situations
Planning future repeat management
Clear advice on whether pollarding is suitable

A considered recommendation

Treevolution considers the species, previous management and the individual site before recommending pollarding.
