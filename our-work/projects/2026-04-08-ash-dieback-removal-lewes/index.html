The job

An ash with dieback, taken down in Lewes.

This large ash had ash dieback and needed to come down. Henry and the Treevolution team dismantled it section by section, as the photographs show.

Instead of taking the whole stem away, enough was left to make something of it. The finished stump became a garden seat — the customer's “Queen B” throne.

“So our big Ash tree had to come down, sadly Ash die back so would end up completely unstable. The end result a throne for, as Alastair said his ‘Queen B’. Thank you Henry Brown and gang from Treevolution for all your hard work — looks fabulous.”
